# Electrical Isolation & Test-Before-Touch Procedure

Applies to any work on or near electrical circuits (switchboard work, fixed
appliance replacement, lighting, hot-water systems, fault finding).

## Licensing
- Only a licensed electrician may perform electrical work. Confirm the
  worker holds a current electrical licence in the job's state before dispatch.
- A general trade worker may **not** disconnect/reconnect fixed wiring.

## Lock-out / Tag-out (LOTO)
1. Identify all energy sources for the equipment.
2. Isolate at the switchboard and apply a personal danger lock + tag.
3. **Test before touch**: prove the tester on a known live source, test the
   isolated circuit is dead, then prove the tester again.
4. Only then commence work. The worker who applied the lock removes it.

## Hazards
- Treat every conductor as live until proven dead.
- For solar PV, DC remains live in daylight even when AC is isolated —
  isolate at the array.
- Report any deteriorated insulation, scorching, or water ingress as a
  safety defect on the work order before proceeding.
