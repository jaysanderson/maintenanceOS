# Working at Heights Safety Policy

Applies to any task performed 2.0 m or more above a lower level (roof work,
gutter cleaning, ceiling repairs, signage, two-storey window work).

## Before the job
- A current **Working at Heights** competency is required for every worker
  on the task. Check the worker's skill record before dispatch.
- Complete a site-specific risk assessment (SWMS) covering access, edge
  protection, weather, and overhead/underground services.
- Inspect all height equipment (ladders, harnesses, anchor points, EWP)
  and tag out any defective item.

## On site
- Prefer passive fall protection (guardrails, scaffolding) over fall-arrest.
- Where fall-arrest is used, anchor points must be rated to 15 kN and a
  rescue plan must be in place before work starts.
- Do not work at heights in winds above 35 km/h or during electrical storms.
- Maintain three points of contact on ladders; never stand on the top two rungs.

## Documentation
- Record the SWMS, equipment inspection, and worker sign-on against the
  work order before commencing.
- Photograph edge protection / anchor setup as a before-photo attachment.
