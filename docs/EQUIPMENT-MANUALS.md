# MaintenanceOS Fleet & Equipment Care Notes

Short maintenance/service guidance for the fleet and equipment, used by the
in-app Fleet/asset service co-pilot. Practical, not exhaustive.

## Service vans (light commercial)

Service every 10,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first. Common symptoms and
checks: a pull to one side or a steering shimmy at speed usually means wheel
alignment or a wheel-bearing/balance issue — inspect tyres for uneven wear and
check bearings before road-testing. Vibration under braking points to warped
discs. Keep registration current; an expired rego takes the vehicle off the road.
Log the odometer at each service.

## Pressure washers

Service the pump seals annually and after heavy use. Loss of pressure is usually
a blocked nozzle, a worn nozzle, or air in the inlet — clear/replace the nozzle
and check the inlet filter first. Never run the pump dry. Store with pump
protector over winter to avoid seal damage.

## Power tools and battery equipment

Battery packs lose runtime as they age; a pack that drops off quickly under load
is near end-of-life and should be replaced, not repaired. Keep air vents clear;
overheating shortens motor life. Tag out any tool with a damaged lead or cracked
housing until repaired — do not use.

## Ladders, harnesses and height equipment

Inspect harnesses and lanyards before every use; retire any with cuts, fraying or
a deployed shock-absorber. Ladders with bent stiles or missing feet are removed
from service. Height equipment has a hard service/inspection interval — do not
extend it. See the Working at Heights safety policy for the controls that apply.

## Trailers

Check tyre pressure, lights and the coupling before each tow. Service wheel
bearings annually; a hot hub or a rumble at speed means a bearing is failing —
do not tow until repaired. Keep the load within the rated capacity.
