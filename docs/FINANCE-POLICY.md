# MaintenanceOS Finance Policy — Accounts Payable & Receivable

This policy governs how supplier bills (AP) and customer invoices (AR) are
validated, matched, approved and chased. The in-app Exception assistant cites
these rules when it explains a finance exception.

## 3-way match and PO tolerance

Every supplier bill should be matched to its purchase order (the "3-way match":
purchase order, goods received, supplier bill). A bill is considered matched when
its total agrees with the linked purchase-order total within tolerance. The
tolerance is the greater of **$50** or **2%** of the PO total. A variance over
tolerance must be investigated before the bill is approved for payment: confirm
quantities received, check for an approved change to the order, and raise a
supplier query if the supplier has over-billed. Do not approve an
over-tolerance bill without documenting the reason.

## Bills with no purchase order

A supplier bill with no linked purchase order cannot be 3-way matched. Where a
single open purchase order for that supplier matches the bill amount within
tolerance, link it. Otherwise the bill must be confirmed as an approved non-PO
charge (for example freight, a call-out fee or a service the company agreed to
without raising a PO) by an authorised approver before payment.

## Disputed supplier bills

A bill marked DISPUTED must not be paid. The dispute must be resolved — or a
supplier credit received — before the bill is approved. Record the reason for the
dispute and the resolution.

## Overdue items

Overdue supplier bills should be paid or queried promptly to avoid supplier
holds and late fees. Overdue customer invoices should be chased with an
escalating reminder: a polite reminder around the due date, a firmer reminder at
roughly two weeks overdue, and a final notice at around 45 days. Consider placing
further work on hold for chronic non-payers.

## Approval thresholds

Routine corrections (for example a cost-centre fix or linking a PO) below
**$5,000** may be approved by a senior AP clerk. Tolerance exceptions and
corrections at or above $5,000 require controller sign-off. Every approval and
every change is recorded in the audit log.

## Missing dates

Bills and invoices must carry an issue date and a due date. Missing dates break
ageing and payment scheduling and must be completed from the source document.
